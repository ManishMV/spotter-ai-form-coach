# AI Form Coach — Product Brief

## Who
Gym-goers aged 18–30 who train without a personal trainer and learn form from YouTube.

## Pain
Nobody checks their form, so they face higher injury risk and slower progress.
Today they use mirrors, film themselves, or ask friends — all inconsistent.

## Why now
Pose-tracking AI now runs free inside any phone browser. No app to install, no cost,
and the video never leaves the device.

## 10-star experience
A real-time coach for every exercise that adapts to your body.

## MVP (v1, due Nov 20)
- Opens from a web link — nothing to install.
- Two exercises: squat and push-up.
- Live rep counting.
- 2–3 form cues per exercise.
- Spoken feedback.

## Anti-goals (not in v1)
- No workout logging
- No accounts
- No nutrition
- No paid AI APIs
- No extra hardware
- No video uploads

## Success metrics
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Rep-count accuracy | 90%+ |
| Form-cue accuracy | Flags real mistakes, few false alarms |
| Device performance | Runs smoothly on X of Y test devices |
| Tester engagement | Testers who finish a set and say they'd use it again |
